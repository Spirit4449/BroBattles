One life each. No respawns. Duels is a 1v1 or 2v2 fight to knock out the other side.

Go solo to settle a rivalry, or bring a teammate who might save your next bad jump. Both formats share the same four maps: Lushy Peaks, Sky Soil, Serenity and Candy Land.

## Sudden death

Take too long and **poison rises from the floor**. That platform won't stay safe forever. Keep climbing and finish the fight.

## Win more Duels

- **Respect your one life.** Getting knocked out early leaves your teammate alone, so back off when you're low.
- **Dash to reposition.** {key:Space} has a cooldown and doesn't make you invulnerable. Save it to dodge a super, not to run in.
- **Hold your super.** Hits charge it. Fire it with {mouse:right} when the other side can't easily move out of the way.
- **Fight for height.** Higher platforms give you better angles, and they matter even more once the poison starts.

> [!NOTE]
> In 2v2, a knocked-out teammate stays out for the rest of the match. Stick close and focus the same target.
