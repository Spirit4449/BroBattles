Three players on each side, one vault to protect. Empty theirs before they empty yours.

**Bank Bust unlocks at 250 peak trophies.** Hits knock coins out of the enemy vault; gold pickups and mines refill yours. You respawn after a knockout, so get back in there.

Turrets and walls cost vault coins. Spend carefully, fight over the coin crates in the middle, and please leave someone guarding home.

Clock runs out? The richer vault wins. [Here’s the full rundown](/help/modes-and-maps).
