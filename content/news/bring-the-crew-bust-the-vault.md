Three players on each side, one vault to protect. Empty theirs before they empty yours.

**Bank Bust unlocks at {trophies:250} peak trophies.** Hits knock coins out of the enemy vault; gold pickups and mines refill yours. You respawn after a knockout, so get back in there.

## Spend to defend

Turrets and walls cost vault coins, so every build is coins you're not banking. Walls block players on both teams, but your own team can shoot through them. A purchase is refused if it would take your vault below its reserve.

## Know Iron Junction

- **Three routes.** Cross on the main lane, the overhead catwalks, or the lower tunnel.
- **The balcony perch** above each vault gives attackers a shot through the hatch. A wall at the hatch shuts that down.
- **The coin crate** is announced and drops on the middle platform every 50 seconds. Either team can break it and grab the coins.

> [!TIP]
> Split the work: two raiders, one guard. Rotate to the crate together when it's announced.
