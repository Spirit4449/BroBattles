The Bro Battles beta is open in your browser. Pick a Bro, choose a mode, and hit Ready.

No download, no install. Open the lobby and you're in as a guest, ready to fight.

## What's in the beta

- **Six Bros.** Ninja and Thorg are ready from the start. Draven, Ozzy, Sparrow and Gloop unlock with gems, and Gloop is also a Trophy Road reward at {trophies:2000}.
- **Two modes.** Duels is 1v1 or 2v2 with one life each. Bank Bust is a 3v3 vault raid with respawns that unlocks at {trophies:250}.
- **Five arenas.** Lushy Peaks, Sky Soil, Serenity and Candy Land host Duels. Bank Bust is fought on Iron Junction.

## Your first match

1. Pick a Bro in the [lobby](/). Every Bro has a different attack and super, so try a few.
2. Leave the mode on **Duels 1v1** for your first fight.
3. Press **Ready**. The first time, you'll accept the Terms and Privacy Policy.
4. Move with {key:A} {key:D}, jump with {key:W}, dash with {key:Space}. Attack with {mouse:left} and fire your super with {mouse:right} once it's charged.

> [!TIP]
> Guest sessions are temporary. [Create an account](/help/accounts) from the same browser to keep your Bros, trophies and coins.

## Earn as you play

Wins earn trophies. Climb the Trophy Road for coins, gems, new modes and cosmetics. Coins level your Bros up, all the way to level 10, for more health and damage.

This is a beta, so things will change and some things will break. Found a weird hit or a broken menu? [Tell us what happened](/feedback). For account or purchase trouble, [contact support](/help/contact). Still learning the buttons? The [controls guide](/help/controls) has you covered.
