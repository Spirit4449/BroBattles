Real-money currency packs require a permanent account. The shop shows the pack and price before payment; Stripe handles checkout. Check the account and price before confirming, and only use a payment method you're authorized to use.

## Payment completed, but currency is missing

Wait a moment and reopen the lobby.

> [!WARNING]
> Don't buy the pack again just to test it. If it's still missing, [contact support](/help/contact) with your username, pack name, purchase date, and receipt or order reference. Never send card numbers, passwords, or verification codes.

## Payment declined or checkout interrupted

Check the checkout message and transaction status before trying again. Your bank or payment provider can explain a decline. If you closed checkout, check your balance before starting a new payment; a temporary authorization isn't always a completed charge.

## Refunds and unexpected purchases

Contact support as soon as you notice a problem. Refund rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law. A refund or chargeback can remove the currency granted by the purchase, and a balance may become negative if it has already been spent. For an unauthorized purchase, also contact the payment-method owner or provider promptly.
