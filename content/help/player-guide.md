A few small habits can make fights easier to read. Pick one to practice for a few matches instead of trying to change everything at once.

## Move with an exit in mind

Change direction as an opponent aims, use platforms to approach from another height, and look for a safe landing before jumping. Dash can get you out of a bad spot, but it has a cooldown and won't make you invulnerable.

## Don't spend every attack at once

Watch where your opponent likes to land, then aim there. Keep an attack ready when you expect them to come close. Your special is useful when charged, but you don't have to use it immediately.

## Help your team

In Duels, surviving gives your teammate more time to help. In Bank Bust, check your vault before chasing a fight across the map. Sometimes defending or collecting coins matters more than another knockout.

If aiming feels uncomfortable, adjust **Mouse sensitivity** in Settings. See [Controls](/help/controls) for the full input list.
