A permanent account lets you add friends, send direct messages, and invite friends to your party. Guests can still play and use party chat, but need to [create an account](/help/accounts) to use the friends list.

## Add a friend

Open {path:Friends > Add}. Share your friend code or enter your friend's code to send a request. You can also find players you've recently played with in the suggestions. The other player must accept the request.

The friends list shows when friends are online and, depending on their privacy settings, when they were last seen. Open a friend's chat to send a direct message. To remove someone, use the remove button beside their name.

## Invite someone to your party

Create or join a party, then use the invite button beside an online friend. They can choose whether to join. If the button isn't available, check that they're online and accept party invitations. Invitations also have a cooldown.

## Choose who can reach you

In {path:Settings > Privacy}, choose who can send friend requests, whether friends can send messages or party invites, whether friends see your last-seen time, and whether you appear in suggestions. Read receipts are shared only when both people allow them. You can also change the friend-request setting in the Friends panel's Add tab.

If a request or message isn't working, check both players' privacy settings and connection. For party links and public parties, see [Play with friends](/help/parties).
