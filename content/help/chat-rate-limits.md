Sending messages or reactions too quickly can trigger a short cooldown. Wait for the notice to clear, then try again. Your draft is kept while the composer is temporarily disabled.

Limits apply to your account across party and battle chat, so changing tabs, parties, or devices won't bypass them. Repeatedly hitting the limit can lead to warnings and a temporary chat suspension. The interface shows the remaining time when available.

If a restriction remains after its displayed time has passed, [contact support](/help/contact) with your username, the approximate time, where you were chatting, and the notice you saw. Don't include passwords, cookies, or session tokens. Don't use another account to evade a restriction.
