Choose a mode, team size, and map, then press Ready. Matchmaking looks for players in the same selection and starts with opponents close to your trophy count. For a party, it uses the group's average trophies and keeps the group together.

The search widens over time if a close match isn't available, so the trophy gap can be larger when fewer people are waiting. Fighter level, character choice, and teamwork can still affect how a match plays out.

If the search takes a while, check that your party is connected and ready. You can cancel the search and try a different playable mode, map, or team size. A mode marked Coming Soon can't be queued.
