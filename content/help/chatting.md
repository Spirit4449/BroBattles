Use party chat to talk in the lobby and battle chat to coordinate during a match. Keep passwords, verification codes, payment details, and other private information out of messages.

## Party chat

Join or create a party, then select **Chat** in the lobby. Current party members can see the conversation. You can reply to a message, add a reaction, and see read and typing indicators. Leaving the party removes your access to its chat.

## Battle chat

Press **/** or select **Chat** to open the composer. Choose **Team** to message your teammates or **All Players** to message the whole match. Press **Enter** to send, **Shift + Enter** for a new line, and **Escape** to close the composer. The match continues while you type. Battle chat is hidden on touch devices.

## Direct messages

Permanent accounts can message friends from the Friends panel. See [Friends and direct messages](/help/friends) for invitations and privacy settings.

If a message won't send, check your connection and make sure you're still in the party or match. If you've sent several messages quickly, wait and try again; see [Chat limits](/help/chat-rate-limits). To report harassment, choose **Safety** in [Contact us](/help/contact).
