Pick a Bro in the [lobby](/), choose a mode and map, and press **Ready**. When a match is found, follow the on-screen prompts and keep the tab open while the arena loads.

> [!TIP]
> You can play as a guest, but the session is temporary. [Create an account](/help/accounts) from the same session to keep your progress.

## Pick a mode

Start with **Duels** if you want to learn movement and attacks. Play 1v1 or team up for 2v2. In **Bank Bust**, two teams of three attack each other's vault while defending their own. Bank Bust requires 250 peak trophies to select yourself; you can also join a party whose owner has unlocked it.

Modes marked **Coming Soon** cannot be played yet, even if you have reached their trophy milestone. See [Modes and maps](/help/modes-and-maps) for the rules.

## Before your first match

You'll be asked to review the Terms and Privacy Policy the first time you press Ready. Accept them to continue. You can adjust sound, sensitivity, and keyboard bindings in Settings. See [Controls](/help/controls) for the defaults and touch controls.

In a match, watch where you land and leave yourself room to dodge. Your attacks and special ability work differently for each Bro, so try a few and see who feels right.
