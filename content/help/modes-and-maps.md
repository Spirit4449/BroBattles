The mode decides how you win. The map decides where the fight happens. Pick both in the lobby before you ready up.

## Duels

Duels is an elimination battle in **1v1, 2v2, or 3v3**. Knock out the other side before your team is eliminated. There are no respawns, so surviving and helping a teammate escape can matter as much as chasing one more hit.

If a Duel stays tied, sudden death pushes the action toward a finish.

## Bank Bust

Bank Bust is a **3v3 objective battle**. Break the enemy vault before they break yours. Players respawn, so a knockout costs time and space rather than ending your match.

A strong team changes gears: defend when your vault is under pressure, then push when the other side is recovering. Fighting well helps, but the vault decides the winner.

## Learning a map

Each arena has its own platforms, gaps, routes, and places to recover. In your first few rounds, notice:

- where you can change height quickly;
- which platforms give you room to dodge;
- where a missed jump becomes dangerous; and
- the shortest safe route back to your team or objective.
