Use [Contact us](/help/contact) when you need help with your account, a purchase, a safety concern, or another issue that needs a reply. A permanent account is required for support conversations. If you can't sign in, email **support@brobattles.dev** instead.

## Send a useful request

Choose the closest category and describe what happened. Include your username, the approximate time, and any relevant details, such as the mode, item name, or order reference. Don't send passwords, verification codes, session tokens, or card details. Attachments aren't supported.

Open [My requests](/help/requests) to read responses and reply. Check there for updates; support replies aren't sent as email notifications.

## Have an idea or found a bug?

Use [Feedback](/feedback) for suggestions and reproducible bugs. Include the steps you took and what you expected to happen. Guests can submit feedback, but guest feedback does not provide a reply channel. If you need individual help, use Contact us instead.
