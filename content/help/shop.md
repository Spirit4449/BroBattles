Open {path:Lobby > Shop} to see available offers. The price and included items are shown before you confirm. Some offers rotate, so an item that isn't there today may appear again later.

## Sales and bundles

The Sales section includes featured and rotating offers. A sale item shows its regular and discounted price; the purchase confirmation shows what you'll pay. Bundles list their contents. Some bundles require a particular included item to be unowned, so check the offer before buying.

Daily and weekly selections change on the schedule shown in the shop. A **NEW** badge highlights offers you haven't viewed; opening a section and closing the shop clears the relevant badges.

## Currency and missing items

Coins and gems can be earned in battles and on Trophy Road. The shop also offers currency packs for real money to permanent accounts. See [Purchase help](/help/purchases) for payment problems and refunds.

After buying a cosmetic, equip it from its collection; buying it doesn't necessarily equip it. If it doesn't appear, check that you're on the right account and reopen the lobby. Contact support with the item name and any order reference if it's still missing. See [Skins and cosmetics](/help/skins) for where to find your collection.
