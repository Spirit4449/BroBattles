Each Bro starts at level 1 and can be upgraded separately to level 10. Upgrades increase health and attack strength; later levels have smaller increases than earlier ones.

## Upgrade a Bro

1. Open your profile or the fighter selection.
2. Choose a Bro you own and select the upgrade option.
3. Review the coin cost and stat changes, then confirm.

Level 2 costs {coins:150} and each level costs more than the last, up to {coins:5800} for level 10. Upgrades cannot be undone or transferred to another Bro, so check your choice before confirming. You need to unlock a Bro before upgrading them.

## How much stronger?

Levels 2 to 5 each add 15% of the Bro's level-1 health and damage. Levels 6 to 10 each add 7.5%, so a level-10 Bro has roughly double the health and damage they started with.

> [!TIP]
> Pick one or two Bros you enjoy and level them first. Spreading coins thin makes every fighter a little stronger instead of one a lot stronger.

Levels help, but they don't change your controls or replace good positioning. See [Rewards and trophies](/help/battle-rewards) for ways to earn coins.
