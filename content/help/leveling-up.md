Each fighter has their own level. A fighter starts at level 1 and can reach **level 10**, the current maximum.

## What a level changes

Leveling increases that fighter’s health and attack strength. Levels 6 through 10 still make a difference, but their increases are gentler so a practiced lower-level player can compete.

Leveling does not change your movement controls, shorten every cooldown, or automatically win a matchup. Timing, positioning, and knowing your fighter still do the heavy lifting.

## How to upgrade

1. Open your profile or fighter selection.
2. Choose an unlocked fighter.
3. Select the upgrade option and review the coin cost.
4. Confirm the upgrade.

Each level costs more coins than the last. You cannot undo a level or move it to another fighter, so spend on the characters you enjoy playing.

Locked fighters must be unlocked before they can be upgraded. Every fighter levels separately.
