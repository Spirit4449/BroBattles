# Duck sound sources

The game uses these Creative Commons 0 recordings:

- `duck-transition.mp3`: “Clothes Rustling Movement 13_9” by Joao_Janz, Freesound sound 493417. https://freesound.org/people/Joao_Janz/sounds/493417/
- `duck-down.wav` and `duck-up.wav`: “Crouching and getting up-dress.wav” by usamah, Freesound sound 464995. https://freesound.org/people/usamah/sounds/464995/
- `duck-block.wav`: “shield guard” by nekoninja, Freesound sound 370203. https://freesound.org/people/nekoninja/sounds/370203/

All source pages identify the recordings as CC0. Gameplay copies were trimmed,
gain-adjusted, and faded where needed to make the effects short and responsive.
