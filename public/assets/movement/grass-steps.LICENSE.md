The source pages describe soft footsteps in grass and explicitly release the recordings under CC0. Playback volume and pitch are adjusted at runtime.

The `*-normalized.ogg` and `*-normalized.mp3` files are game-ready derivatives made from the corresponding untouched downloads using FFmpeg loudness normalization (`I=-20`, `LRA=7`, `TP=-2`). Opus-in-OGG is preferred because it avoids stale ReplayGain metadata embedded in the source MP3s; normalized MP3 remains as a compatibility fallback. The untouched source renditions remain beside them for provenance.
